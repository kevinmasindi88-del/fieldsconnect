# Single agreement — signup acceptance QA

- Keep ONE checkbox labelled: I agree to the FieldsConnect Terms & Conditions and Code of Conduct.
- Read more expands complete Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Community Guidelines links.
- The signup form collects an actual name, career/educational category and field of interest alongside email and password; this all counts as step 1.
- Step 2 is email verification. There must be no mandatory post-verification profile setup.
- Profile data is saved to a new private profile after verified sign-in, and existing account records remain unchanged.
- RLS and agreement audit are retained; no new 18+ declaration is made.
- Legal guardian/child data requirements remain separate from the ordinary signup checkbox.
- End-to-end verification, mobile rendering and two-user access tests required before promotion.
