# Age consent signup update — 25 October 2026

FieldsConnect ordinary registration keeps a self-declared 18+ checkbox. When unticked, it opens inline parent/guardian fields for name, date of birth and email, with affirmative approval. No separate page, no adult DOB requirement and no unrequested explanation about other providers or verification processes.

The guardian attestation is written into restricted database records at account creation and is never published as profile data. This records the submitted declaration but is not an independent check of adult identity or authority. Secure storage, privacy notice and operational/legal review remain essential.

Member profiles are private by default on first login. Existing site registration, email verification and Terms acceptance remain intact. The QA checklist addresses field validation and end-to-end testing before production.
