## Editorial and version control
- Only the public Community Guidelines may be linked as FieldsConnect's Code of Conduct from signup.
- Before changing a member obligation or consequence, review the Terms, Privacy Policy, library terms, active moderation implementation and relevant statutory rights together.
- Communicate material changes and use a new policy agreement version and appropriate renewed acceptance when necessary.
- Do not publish moderator access controls, internal case-processing notes, product development status, legal checklists or technical instructions as part of the public Code.
- No provision should promise an automated enforcement feature, identity verification, guardian consent, fixed response time or dispute outcome unless the implementation and operational process support it.
- A report is evidence to review, not proof of a violation. Enforcement must be proportional and documented.
